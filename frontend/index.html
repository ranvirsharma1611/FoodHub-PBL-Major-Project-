Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my delivery rider in real time?

Once your order is placed, click on "Menu" -> "My Orders" or view the Live Order Tracker modal to see real-time updates and contact your assigned rider.

What is FoodHub's average delivery time?

Our partner restaurants prepare food fresh in 15-20 minutes, and our delivery partners deliver within 30 minutes in thermal insulated bags.

How do refunds and cancellations work?

Cancellations are accepted before the kitchen begins preparation. In accordance with our refund policy, refunds are automatically returned to your original payment method within 2-4 business hours.

Can I apply multiple coupons on one order?

You can apply 1 promo coupon code per order, plus you can redeem your FoodHub Rewards points for free dishes simultaneously!
