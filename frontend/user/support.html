Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers to common customer questions

How can I track my food delivery rider in real time?

Visit the "Live Order Tracker" page or click on the tracking banner in your dashboard to view the delivery progress timeline and assigned rider contact info.

How do refunds and order cancellations work?

Orders can be cancelled before kitchen preparation starts. If approved, digital payments are refunded automatically to the original method in 2 hours.

Can I redeem FoodHub reward points on any order?

Yes! You earn 18 reward points per ₹100 spent. Points can be redeemed for free Molten Choco Lava Cakes, thickshakes, or flat discount vouchers.
